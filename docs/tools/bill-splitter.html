Bill splitter

Paste a receipt in any format, check what was read, then split it between people. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

How it works
  • Copy the text of a receipt (Toast, Square, an email, or just notes like burger 12) and paste it. The reader finds item lines, the subtotal, tax, tip and total, and skips card and payment lines.
  • It checks the numbers add up. If they don't, it tries other readings, such as a quantity line being a per-unit price or an add-on already included, and keeps the one that fits.
  • Lines with an amount it didn't use are listed so you can add them as items with one click.
  • Each person pays their items' share of the items sum × the total, so tax, tip and fees are split in proportion. An item given to several people is divided evenly between them. Amounts are rounded so they add up to the total exactly.
