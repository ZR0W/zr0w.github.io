Tilebound sound effects
=======================

The recorded clips in this folder are short, trimmed and processed excerpts (mono, 32 kHz, faded, peak-normalized)
from the Sonniss.com GDC 2024 Game Audio Bundle, used under the Sonniss GDC bundle license (royalty-free use in
projects; the original library may not be redistributed). The original, unprocessed files are not in this repository.
Every other effect in the game is synthesized in code (docs/js/tilebound-sfx.js).
